# The desktop

## The menu bar

At the very top:

- **The Axia DE logo:** About, Settings, Apps, lock the screen, switch user and log out.
- **Reopening windows:** the apps open when you log out, restart or shut down come back in place at the next login. After a power cut Axia DE asks first. Choose Always, Ask first or Off in Settings → Session.
- **The menus of the app in front** (for example File, Edit, View), plus the Window and Help menus.
- **On the right:** status icons (like the Clock timer or next alarm), the privacy indicators
  (camera, microphone, screen), the keyboard layout, the Search magnifier, **Control Center**
  and the date and time.

## The Dock and Apps

- The **Dock** shows pinned and running apps (a dot underneath). Right-click an icon for its options.
- **Apps** (**F4** or tap the ❖ key alone), the app launcher, shows every app by category, with search: Axia DE
  apps, apps installed from the Store and Linux apps.

## Control Center

The network (and active VPNs), sound, dark mode, transparency, accent color, wallpaper, memory and
processor, and what's playing. Network and sound open their Settings section.

## Wallpaper and widgets

Change the wallpaper in [Settings → Wallpaper](settings:wallpaper). Notes from the
[Notes](help:notes) app can stay on the desktop as sticky notes.

## Themes

Axia DE's desktop is **Lord Flow** (menu bar and Dock). A theme installed from elsewhere shows up in
[Settings → Appearance](settings:appearance), which switches to it live.

Used to the keys of Linux or Windows desktops? Choose **Linux / Windows** in
[Settings → Keyboard](settings:keyboard): Alt+Tab switches apps, Alt+F4 closes the window in front,
Super+arrows snap it and Super+D shows the desktop ([Keyboard shortcuts](help:shortcuts)).
