# Appearance and themes

- **Dark or light mode:** [Settings → Appearance](settings:appearance), or Control Center.
- **Accent color:** the one used by buttons, selections and switches.
- **Transparency:** bars and menus with blur. Turning it off saves work on slow computers.
- **Wallpaper:** [Settings → Wallpaper](settings:wallpaper).
- **Desktop style:** Axia DE's desktop is **Lord Flow** (menu bar on top and Dock). When another
  theme is installed, [Settings → Appearance](settings:appearance) switches the whole desktop to it
  live, without closing apps: the open windows come back in the new style.

Every app takes the theme's colors, including those installed from the Store.
